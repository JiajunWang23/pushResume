# PushResume

**AI-powered resume builder that writes, scores, matches, and edits your resume — so you don't have to.**

🔗 [pushresume.net](https://pushresume.net)

---

## What It Does

PushResume automates the entire resume workflow from scratch to submission-ready.

- **Generate from text** — Paste your experience in plain text and get a professionally formatted resume instantly. No templates, no formatting work.
- **Upload existing resume** — Already have one? Upload it directly. The tool analyzes and optimizes from where you are.
- **ATS scan & scoring** — Simulates how applicant tracking systems read your resume. Know exactly what's getting filtered before it reaches a recruiter.
- **Auto JD matching** — Paste any job description. The tool compares it against your resume, identifies gaps, and generates targeted suggestions.
- **AI rewrite** — It doesn't just suggest edits. It rewrites your resume for you. You review, you approve, you're done.
- **Overleaf preview** — The final resume opens directly in Overleaf. Clean LaTeX format, export to PDF anytime. No hunting for templates, no manual formatting.

1. Go to [pushresume.net](https://pushresume.net)
