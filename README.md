# 🚀 PushResume  
AI-powered Resume Builder with ATS Optimization & LaTeX Export

> Build, optimize, and share job-winning resumes in minutes.

---

## ✨ Overview

PushResume is an AI-powered resume platform designed to help job seekers create ATS-friendly, recruiter-ready resumes with minimal effort.

Users can upload or input their resume, and PushResume will automatically:
- Analyze resume quality  
- Optimize content for specific job descriptions  
- Generate clean, professional LaTeX-style resumes  

The goal is simple: **help users get more interviews.**

---

## 🔥 Features

### 📄 Smart Resume Builder
- Upload resume (PDF / DOCX / TXT)
- Automatically parse and structure content  
- Clean, professional formatting  

### 🎯 ATS Optimization
- Match resume with job descriptions  
- Identify missing keywords  
- Improve ATS pass rate  

### 🤖 AI Resume Enhancement
- Rewrite bullet points for impact  
- Improve clarity and professionalism  
- Tailor resume to specific roles  

### 🔗 Shareable Resume Link
- Generate a single link for recruiters  
- Avoid sending multiple versions  

### 📊 Resume Scoring
- Instant feedback on resume quality  
- Actionable improvement suggestions  

---

## 🧠 Why PushResume?

Many candidates get rejected not because of lack of skills, but because:
- Their resume fails ATS filters  
- Their content is not tailored  
- Formatting is poor  

PushResume solves this by automating:
- Keyword optimization  
- Content rewriting  
- Professional formatting
